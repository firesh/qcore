# QR Code Benchmark Comparison Summary

Head-to-head evaluation of **`qore`**, **`zxing-cpp`**, **`wechat-qr`**, and **`opencv-qr`** on `arm64` (macOS-27.2-arm64-arm-64bit).

> **Timing methodology**: For all 4 engines, timings measure pure QR detection & decoding on the in-memory image (excluding disk I/O and PNG/JPEG decoding). `qore` runs via a persistent `--batch-stdin --raw --json --multi` subprocess (`--unreadable` enabled for BoofCV detection so unreadable detected quads are counted symmetrically with `wechat-qr` / `opencv-qr`). `--raw` and `cv2.IMREAD_IGNORE_ORIENTATION` ensure raw pixel coordinates align with BoofCV ground truth.
